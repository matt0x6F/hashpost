# Content Creation

## Overview

HashPost provides a comprehensive content creation system that allows users to share posts, engage in discussions, and build communities through pseudonymous interactions.

## What Users Can Do

### Creating Posts

**Start Discussions**
- Create new posts in any subforum you're subscribed to
- Add titles and content to spark discussions
- Generate URL-friendly slugs automatically

**Post Content**
- Write rich text content with markdown support
- Include links and media when appropriate
- Set post visibility and access controls

**Post Management**
- Edit your own posts
- Delete posts you no longer want visible
- Lock posts to prevent new comments
- Make posts sticky for important announcements

### Commenting System

**Join Discussions**
- Comment on any post in accessible subforums
- Reply to other comments to create threaded discussions
- Express opinions and share knowledge

**Comment Features**
- Write comments with markdown formatting
- Reply to specific comments to create threads
- Edit your own comments

**Comment Management**
- Delete comments you no longer want visible
- Report inappropriate comments
- Manage your comment activity

### Voting System

**Engage with Content**
- Upvote content you find valuable or interesting
- Downvote content that doesn't contribute
- Remove votes to change your mind

**Vote Impact**
- Votes affect content visibility and ranking
- High-scoring content appears more prominently
- Community consensus shapes content discovery
- **Karma Impact**: Votes contribute to the author's karma score

**Vote Tracking**
- See your voting history
- Track how your votes affect content ranking
- Understand voting patterns in communities

> **📚 Learn More**: For detailed information about how voting affects karma and reputation, see [Karma System](../karma.md).

## User Experience

### Creating a Post

1. **Choose a Subforum**
   - Browse available subforums
   - Select the most appropriate community
   - Check subforum rules and guidelines

2. **Write Your Post**
   - Craft an engaging title
   - Write clear, informative content
   - Use markdown for formatting

3. **Submit and Share**
   - Preview your post before submitting
   - Submit to the community
   - Engage with responses

### Participating in Discussions

1. **Read and Understand**
   - Read the original post carefully
   - Review existing comments
   - Understand the discussion context

2. **Add Your Perspective**
   - Write thoughtful, relevant comments
   - Reply to specific points or questions
   - Contribute to the conversation

3. **Engage Respectfully**
   - Vote on content you find valuable
   - Respond to replies on your comments
   - Follow community guidelines

## Content Types

### Text Posts

- Use markdown for rich formatting
- Include images or links when relevant

### Link Posts
- Share interesting articles or resources
- Add context or commentary
- Help others discover valuable content

### Discussion Posts
- Ask questions to the community
- Start debates on interesting topics
- Seek advice or recommendations

## Community Guidelines

### Quality Content
- Write clear, informative posts
- Use descriptive titles
- Provide context and background

### Respectful Engagement
- Be civil and constructive
- Avoid personal attacks
- Follow subforum-specific rules

### Spam Prevention
- Don't post the same content repeatedly
- Avoid excessive self-promotion
- Respect community boundaries

## Content Discovery

### Trending Content
- Popular posts appear prominently
- High-scoring content gets more visibility
- Community favorites rise to the top

### New Content
- Recent posts are easily discoverable
- Fresh discussions are highlighted
- New users can find active conversations

### Content Organization
- Posts organized by subforum

### Community Standards
