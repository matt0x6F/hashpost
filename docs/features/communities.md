# Communities & Subforums

## Overview

HashPost communities are organized into subforums - focused spaces where users can discuss specific topics, share knowledge, and build connections around shared interests.

## What Users Can Do

### Discovering Communities

**Browse Subforums**
- Explore available communities by topic
- See subscriber counts and activity levels
- Find communities that match your interests

**Browse Communities**
- View all available subforums
- See subscriber counts and activity levels
- Sort communities by name, subscribers, posts, or creation date

**Community Information**
- Read community descriptions and rules
- See recent activity and popular posts
- Understand community culture and guidelines

### Joining Communities

**Subscribe to Subforums**
- Join communities you're interested in
- Receive updates from your subscribed subforums
- Build your personalized feed

**Community Participation**
- Post and comment in subscribed subforums
- Vote on content within communities
- Engage with other community members

**Community Participation**
- Subscribe to communities you're interested in
- Participate in discussions within communities
- Build connections with community members

### Community Management

**Your Subscriptions**
- Manage your subscribed communities
- Organize communities by interest
- Control your community feed

**Subscription Management**
- Subscribe and unsubscribe from communities
- View your subscribed communities
- Manage your community feed
