# Communities & Subforums

## Overview

HashPost communities are organized into subforums - focused spaces where users can discuss specific topics, share knowledge, and build connections around shared interests. The platform supports four different types of communities, each with its own governance model and purpose.

## Community Types

HashPost supports four distinct community types, each designed for different purposes and governance models:

### 🎯 **Topical Communities (t/)**
**Purpose**: Generic subjects and topics  
**Examples**: `t/programming`, `t/cooking`, `t/books`  
**Governance**: Democratic - Community-elected moderators  
**Features**:
- Open to all users for participation
- Moderators are elected by the community
- Community-driven decision making
- Ideal for general interest topics

### 🌍 **Geographic Communities (g/)**
**Purpose**: Location-based discussions  
**Examples**: `g/seattle`, `g/tacoma`, `g/portland`  
**Governance**: Democratic - Community-elected moderators  
**Features**:
- Focused on specific locations
- Local community building
- Moderators elected by local members
- Great for local events and discussions

### 🏢 **Branded Communities (b/)**
**Purpose**: Company and brand-owned spaces  
**Examples**: `b/apple`, `b/minecraft`, `b/tesla`  
**Governance**: Owned - Company-controlled management  
**Features**:
- Official company presence
- Company-appointed moderators
- Brand-controlled content and policies
- Official announcements and support

### 👤 **Creator Communities (c/)**
**Purpose**: Individual creator-owned spaces  
**Examples**: `c/joerogan`, `c/mrbeast`, `c/lexfridman`  
**Governance**: Owned - Creator-controlled management  
**Features**:
- Creator-owned and managed
- Creator-appointed moderators
- Direct creator engagement
- Fan community building

### Discovering Communities

**Community Information**
- Read community descriptions and rules

### Joining Communities

**Subscribe to Subforums**
- Join communities you're interested in
- Receive updates from your subscribed subforums
- Build your personalized feed across different community types

**Community Participation**
- Post and comment in subscribed subforums
- Vote on content within communities
- Engage with other community members
- Respect the governance model of each community
- Build reputation through karma in each community

### Understanding Governance

**Democratic Communities (t/ and g/)**
- Moderators are elected by community members
- Community-driven decision making
- Open participation in governance
- Transparent moderation processes

**Owned Communities (b/ and c/)**
- Moderators are appointed by the owner
- Owner-controlled content and policies
- Official announcements and guidance
- Brand/creator-driven community culture
