# Moderation & Safety

## Overview

HashPost provides a comprehensive moderation system that helps maintain community quality and safety while preserving user privacy and free expression.

## What Users Can Do

### Reporting Content

**Report Problematic Content**
- Report posts, comments, or users that violate guidelines
- Provide specific reasons for your report
- Help maintain community quality and safety

**Report Categories**
- Harassment or abuse
- Spam or low-quality content
- Misinformation or harmful content
- Violations of community guidelines

**Report Management**
- View reports you've submitted
- Track report status and resolution
- Provide additional context when needed

### Understanding Moderation

**Community Guidelines**
- Each subforum has specific rules
- Platform-wide guidelines apply to all communities
- Clear expectations for user behavior

**Moderation Actions**
- Content can be removed by moderators
- Users can be banned from specific communities
- Appeals process for moderation decisions

**Transparency**
- Moderators explain their decisions
- Clear reasoning for content removal
- Fair process for all users

## User Experience

### Reporting Process

1. **Identify Problematic Content**
   - Recognize content that violates guidelines
   - Consider the impact on the community
   - Decide if reporting is appropriate

2. **Submit a Report**
   - Click the report button on the content
   - Select the appropriate category
   - Provide specific details about the issue

3. **Follow Up**
   - Reports are reviewed by moderators
   - You may be contacted for additional information
   - Respect the moderation process

### Understanding Moderation Decisions

1. **Review the Decision**
   - Moderators provide explanations
   - Understand why content was removed
   - Learn from the moderation process

2. **Appeal if Necessary**
   - If you believe a decision was wrong
   - Provide additional context
   - Respect the appeals process

3. **Learn and Improve**
   - Understand community guidelines better
   - Adjust your behavior accordingly
   - Help maintain community quality
