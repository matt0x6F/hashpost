# Subforum Rules & Configuration System

## Overview

HashPost provides a comprehensive rules system that allows each subforum to have its own customized guidelines and enforcement policies. This system enables moderators and owners to create specific rules for their communities while maintaining consistency and fairness across the platform.

**Status**: Planning

## What Users Can Do

### For Regular Users

**Understanding Community Rules**
- Each subforum displays its specific rules and guidelines
- Rules are clearly visible before you post or comment
- Different communities may have different standards and expectations
- Rules help you understand what's acceptable in each community

**Rule-Based Reporting**
- Report content that violates specific subforum rules
- Reports are automatically categorized based on rule violations
- Clear feedback on why your report was submitted
- Transparent process for rule enforcement

**Rule Compliance**
- See which rules apply to your content before posting
- Get warnings when content might violate rules
- Understand the consequences of rule violations
- Learn from rule violations to improve future contributions

### For Moderators & Owners

**Rule Configuration**
- Create and customize rules for your subforum
- Set different rule categories (content, behavior, spam, safety)
- Configure rule conditions and triggers
- Define automated actions for rule violations

**Rule Management**
- Edit existing rules as community needs change
- Enable or disable rules as needed
- Set rule priorities and enforcement levels
- Track rule effectiveness and usage

**Rule Templates**
- Use pre-built rule templates for common scenarios
- Customize templates to fit your community
- Share effective rules with other moderators
- Learn from successful rule configurations

## User Experience

### For Regular Users

**Before Posting**
1. **Review Rules**: Check the subforum's rules before creating content
2. **Understand Guidelines**: Read through all applicable rule categories
3. **Consider Impact**: Think about how your content might affect the community
4. **Ask Questions**: Contact moderators if you're unsure about rules

**When Reporting**
1. **Identify Violation**: Determine which specific rule was violated
2. **Provide Context**: Explain how the content violates the rule
3. **Submit Report**: Use the reporting system with rule-specific categories
4. **Follow Up**: Check on report status and moderator decisions

**After Rule Violations**
1. **Learn from Feedback**: Understand why your content violated rules
2. **Adjust Behavior**: Modify your approach to comply with guidelines
3. **Ask for Clarification**: Contact moderators if you need rule clarification
4. **Appeal if Necessary**: Appeal decisions you believe were made in error

### For Moderators & Owners

**Setting Up Rules**
1. **Define Community Standards**: Establish what makes your community unique
2. **Choose Rule Categories**: Select appropriate rule types for your subforum
3. **Configure Conditions**: Set specific triggers and conditions for rules
4. **Set Actions**: Define what happens when rules are violated
5. **Test Rules**: Verify rules work as expected before going live

**Managing Rules**
1. **Monitor Effectiveness**: Track how well rules are working
2. **Adjust as Needed**: Modify rules based on community feedback
3. **Communicate Changes**: Inform users when rules are updated
4. **Enforce Consistently**: Apply rules fairly to all users

## Rule Categories

### Content Rules
**Purpose**: Define what types of content are acceptable in the subforum

**Examples**:
- **No self-promotion**: Prohibits excessive self-promotional content
- **Topic relevance**: Ensures posts relate to the subforum's subject
- **Content quality**: Requires meaningful, well-written posts
- **Media guidelines**: Sets standards for images, videos, and links

**User Impact**:
- Clear guidelines on what content is welcome
- Reduced spam and low-quality posts
- Better discussion quality and relevance
- Consistent community experience

### Behavior Rules
**Purpose**: Establish standards for user interactions and behavior

**Examples**:
- **Be respectful**: Requires civil and constructive communication
- **No personal attacks**: Prohibits targeting individuals
- **Constructive criticism**: Encourages helpful feedback
- **Stay on topic**: Keeps discussions focused and relevant

**User Impact**:
- Safer, more welcoming environment
- Reduced harassment and abuse
- Better community relationships
- More productive discussions

### Spam Rules
**Purpose**: Prevent unwanted promotional and repetitive content

**Examples**:
- **No commercial spam**: Prohibits unsolicited promotional content
- **Repetitive posting**: Limits duplicate or similar content
- **Link restrictions**: Controls external link sharing
- **Bot prevention**: Detects and prevents automated spam

**User Impact**:
- Cleaner, more focused discussions
- Less commercial interference
- Better content discovery
- Reduced noise and clutter

### Safety Rules
**Purpose**: Protect users from harmful content and behavior

**Examples**:
- **No harassment**: Prohibits targeting or bullying users
- **Privacy protection**: Prevents sharing personal information
- **No threats**: Prohibits violent or threatening language
- **Age-appropriate content**: Ensures content is suitable for all users

**User Impact**:
- Safer environment for all users
- Protection from harmful content
- Better privacy protection
- Inclusive community atmosphere

### Community Rules
**Purpose**: Subforum-specific guidelines that reflect community culture

**Examples**:
- **Local focus**: Geographic communities may require local relevance
- **Expertise levels**: Technical communities may have skill-based rules
- **Cultural sensitivity**: Communities may have specific cultural guidelines
- **Brand guidelines**: Company communities may have brand-specific rules

**User Impact**:
- Community-specific experience
- Relevant and focused discussions
- Appropriate content for the community
- Stronger community identity

## Rule Actions

### Warning System
**How it Works**:
- Users receive warnings when they violate rules
- Warnings explain what rule was violated and why
- Multiple warnings may lead to more serious consequences
- Warnings help users learn and improve

**User Experience**:
- Clear feedback on rule violations
- Opportunity to correct behavior
- Understanding of community expectations
- Fair warning before serious consequences

### Auto-Removal
**How it Works**:
- Content that clearly violates rules is automatically removed
- Users are notified when their content is removed
- Explanation of which rule was violated
- Option to appeal removal decisions

**User Experience**:
- Immediate feedback on rule violations
- Clear understanding of why content was removed
- Opportunity to learn from mistakes
- Fair appeal process for contested removals

### Temporary Restrictions
**How it Works**:
- Users may be temporarily restricted from posting
- Restrictions are time-limited and clearly communicated
- Users can still read and participate in other ways
- Restrictions lift automatically after the time period

**User Experience**:
- Clear understanding of restriction reasons
- Time-limited consequences for rule violations
- Opportunity to reflect and improve
- Fair and proportional enforcement

### Report Generation
**How it Works**:
- Potential rule violations are automatically flagged
- Reports are created for moderator review
- Moderators can review and take appropriate action
- Users are notified of report outcomes

**User Experience**:
- Automated detection of potential issues
- Consistent rule enforcement
- Moderator oversight of automated decisions
- Transparent reporting process
