# Reporting & Moderation System

## Overview

HashPost provides a comprehensive reporting and moderation system that allows users to help maintain community quality while preserving privacy and ensuring fair treatment for all participants.

**Status**: ✅ **Implemented**

## What Users Can Do

### Reporting Content

**Report Problematic Content**
- Report posts or comments that violate community guidelines
- Access reporting through the three-dot menu on any post or comment
- Provide specific reasons for your report to help moderators understand the issue

**Report Categories**
- **Spam**: Unwanted promotional content or repetitive posts
- **Harassment**: Abusive behavior or targeted attacks
- **Misinformation**: False or misleading information
- **Violations**: Content that breaks community rules
- **Other**: Any other concerns that don't fit the above categories

**Report Process**
1. Click the three-dot menu (⋮) on any post or comment
2. Select "Report" from the dropdown menu
3. Choose the appropriate reason for your report
4. Add any additional details to help moderators understand the issue
5. Submit your report anonymously

### Understanding Report Status

**Report Tracking**
- Your reports are reviewed by community moderators
- Reports are processed in order of submission
- You can see the status of your reports in the moderation interface

**Report Statuses**
- **Pending**: Your report is waiting for moderator review
- **Investigating**: Moderators are actively reviewing the content
- **Resolved**: Action has been taken on the reported content
- **Dismissed**: The report was reviewed but no action was taken

**Moderator Actions**
- **Remove Content**: Posts or comments that violate guidelines are removed
- **Ban User**: Users who repeatedly violate rules may be banned from the community
- **Ban Pseudonym**: Specific pseudonyms may be banned for rule violations
- **Mute User**: Temporary restrictions on user participation
- **Warning**: Official warnings for minor violations

## User Experience

### Making a Report

1. **Identify the Issue**
   - Consider whether the content actually violates guidelines
   - Think about the impact on the community
   - Ensure your report is made in good faith

2. **Submit Your Report**
   - Use the three-dot menu to access the report option
   - Select the most appropriate category
   - Provide clear, specific details about the problem
   - Submit your report and wait for moderator review

3. **Follow Up**
   - Reports are typically reviewed within 24-48 hours
   - Moderators may contact you for additional information
   - Respect the moderation process and decisions

### Understanding Moderation Decisions

**Transparent Process**
- Moderators provide explanations for their decisions
- All moderation actions are logged and tracked
- Users can appeal moderation decisions if they believe they were made in error

**Fair Treatment**
- All users are treated equally under community guidelines
- Moderation decisions are based on clear, consistent criteria
- Privacy is maintained throughout the moderation process
